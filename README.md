# Dr. Steven M. Grimes

Personal website for Dr. Steven M. Grimes, Distinguished Professor Emeritus,
Department of Physics and Astronomy, Ohio University, Athens, Ohio.

Built by Rana Tahir Bilal — https://ranatahirbilal.github.io/portfolio/

The page carries `noindex, nofollow` until Dr. Grimes has reviewed and
approved it.

## Sources for every claim on the page
- Ohio University, Distinguished Professor register (2001, Physics)
- Ohio University news, "Edwards Accelerator Laboratory turns 50", Sept 2021
- S. M. Grimes, T. N. Massey, A. V. Voinov, "Level density rotational
  enhancement factor", Phys. Rev. C 99, 064331 (2019)
- 24-7PressRelease / Marquis Who's Who releases, Nov 2025 and June 2023,
  used only where a university or journal source does not exist
